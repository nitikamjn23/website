Gupta Medical Stores
https://guptamedicalstores.in

A web-based pharmacy ordering platform developed for Gupta Medical Stores, designed to make ordering medicines and managing customer orders simple and convenient.

Features
🛒 Online medicine ordering
📋 Customer registration and order management
💊 Medicine search and order summary
📄 Prescription upload
🚚 Delivery information and order tracking
📧 Email notifications and order confirmations
🔔 Medicine refill/reminder functionality
👨‍⚕️ Doctor appointment information
📱 Responsive and user-friendly interface
Purpose

The website was developed to provide customers with a convenient way to browse medicines, submit prescriptions, place orders, and receive order-related updates while helping the pharmacy streamline its order management process.
